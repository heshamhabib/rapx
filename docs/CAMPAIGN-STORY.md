# RedAlert Project X — Campaign Story

The full story of the "Project X" campaigns, mission by mission. The design behind it (menus, engine work, rollout,
cinematics) is [IDEA-18 in DESIGN-IDEAS.md](DESIGN-IDEAS.md#idea-18--campaign-story-project-x). This doc holds what
happens: the plot, the briefings, the objectives and how each mission plays.

| Campaign | Side | Status |
|----------|------|--------|
| **Operation Chimera** | Union | ✍️ Written (this doc, part 1) |
| **Operation Legion** | Vanguard | ⏳ To write (part 2) |
| **Convergence** (secret) | Both | ⏳ To write (part 3) |

**Rules for the story**
- **Every mission is in a different country** (2026-10-27). No country appears twice in the same campaign.
- Side names are **Union** and **Vanguard** (see the note at the top of DESIGN-IDEAS.md).
- X-Strain is a **fictional** virus. Outbreaks appear as contamination zones on the map and in briefings, never as graphic
  suffering, and no real pandemic or real country's programme is named or blamed.
- Chimera is the work of **Marshal Drozdov's faction**, kept secret even from most of the Vanguard. Ordinary Vanguard
  soldiers are enemies in battle, not the villains of the story. This keeps the Vanguard campaign and the
  Convergence handshake believable.
- Briefing lines below are first drafts for the voice-over and on-screen text; trim them to fit 20–40 s clips.

# Part 1 — Union campaign: Operation Chimera

> *2026. The Cold War never ended; it just changed names. The **Union** and the **Vanguard** have watched each other
> across every border for eighty years. Each side calls the other's secret weapon programme **"Project X"**.
> Union intelligence has found the Vanguard's: a man-made virus, X-Strain, and a launch date twelve days away.*

## 1. The story in one page

Union intelligence intercepts a Vanguard shipping schedule marked **CHIMERA** and a countdown: **Day Zero** is twelve days
away. General Eleanor Hart gives the Commander one task: find out what Chimera is, and stop it.

A cargo ship in the Bosphorus leads to a name, **Dr. Irina Volkova**, the virologist who designed X-Strain. Volkova
wants out; the Union pulls her through the streets of Vienna. She tells them what Chimera really is: a virus grown in a
vault deep underground, code name **Kolodets — "the Well"** — and the Vanguard Marshal behind it, **Viktor Drozdov**.

The Union follows the virus around the world. Samples captured in Egypt carry a hidden signature Volkova recognises: the
initials of her dead mentor, **Professor Elias Varga**. Missile boats in Havana show the virus is meant to fly, not
sail. A field test near Agra goes wrong, and Union and Indian troops contain the first outbreak side by side. In Tokyo
the Commander holds off Drozdov's strike long enough for Volkova to finish a vaccine, but a vaccine takes months to make
and Day Zero is days away. The source itself must die.

A Vanguard data centre in the hills north of Beijing gives up the Well's location: **400 metres under a mountain
in Iran's Zagros range**, dug in secret beneath an old mine, ringed by dispersal missile silos and the strongest air defence the
Vanguard owns.
No tank can reach it and no ordinary bomb can break it. Only one weapon can: the Union's **AB-2 Wraith** stealth bomber,
carrying the **Earthbreaker** deep-penetrator bomb.

The Commander seizes a forward air base in the Gobi for the Wraiths, then storms Moscow to capture Drozdov and the codes
to the Vanguard's air defence grid. Drozdov talks: he never invented Chimera. The research was **given** to him by
someone who signs as **"the Architect"**.

On **Day Zero** the Commander lands in the Zagros, blinds the air defence grid, shoots down the dispersal missiles as
they launch, and paints the Well's shafts for the bombers. Two Wraiths come in through the dark. The first Earthbreaker
cracks the vault; the second, a **Sunfire** thermal bomb, burns everything inside so nothing can escape. The mountain sinks
in on itself. Chimera is dead.

In the ruins, Major Moreau finds a terminal still sending data to an unknown island. The last file is signed by the
Architect, and its attachments are Union design documents for a robot army called **LEGION** and its AI, **ARGUS**.
Somebody has been building both sides of this war.

## 5. Prologue (campaign intro cinematic)

A Union war room at night. Satellite photos on a light table: a cargo ship, a snowfield, a circle of silo doors.

> **Hart:** "Three days ago a Vanguard courier was arrested in Athens. He was carrying a shipping schedule, a word, and a
> date. The word is *Chimera*. The date is twelve days from now. Our scientists read the schedule twice and asked to read
> it again. They think Chimera is a disease, Commander. Built, not born. And on that date, someone intends to let it out.
> Twelve days. Find it."

Title card: **OPERATION CHIMERA — DAY 12**.

## 6. Act I — The Trail (Day 12–10)

### A1 — Strait of Shadows
**Day 12 · Istanbul, Turkey · Briefing: Hart**

**Story.** The courier's schedule names a ship: the Vanguard freighter *Akademik Sorokin*, sailing south through the
Bosphorus from the Black Sea with "agricultural equipment". If it reaches the Mediterranean it disappears. A small Union
task force waits in the Sea of Marmara.

> **Hart:** "The *Sorokin* passes under the Bosphorus bridges at dawn. I want that ship stopped, not sunk. Whatever she's
> carrying, I want it on my desk, and I'd rather it didn't end up at the bottom of the strait."

**Objectives**
- Build a base on the Asian shore (an MCV lands with you).
- Destroy the three coastal gun batteries guarding the narrows.
- **Disable** the *Sorokin* (bring her below 30 % health; sinking her fails the mission).
- Move an Engineer onto the ship to seize the cargo manifest.
- *Bonus:* capture the old fortress on the European shore (a landmark that gives sight over the strait).

**How it plays.** The tutorial-paced opener: base building and a small navy. The *Sorokin* moves south on a fixed
path with escorts; the player has about 15 minutes before she clears the map. Destroyers and gunboats harass the
escorts while ground forces knock out the batteries. Easy and Normal slow the ship down.

**Ending.** The manifest lists bioreactors and cold-storage vaults, all signed off by **Dr. I. Volkova**, with a delivery
address that is only a code: **KOLODETS**. Moreau's voice cuts in: *"Volkova. I know that name. She's in Vienna next
week for a conference. She asked a colleague of mine, very quietly, whether the Union still grants asylum."*

**New:** base building, navy, capturing with engineers. **Thread:** the countdown starts.

### A2 — The Defector
**Day 11 · Vienna, Austria · Briefing: Moreau**

**Story.** Vienna is neutral ground. Volkova slips away from her escorts during a conference dinner, but Drozdov's
security troops, the **Black Frost** detachment, are already sweeping the old city for her.

> **Moreau:** "No tanks, no base, no noise. Austria is neutral and would like to stay that way. You have a commando, two
> of my agents and a boat waiting on the Danube. Doctor Volkova is waiting in a café near the Opera. Try not to let her
> get shot before she tells us anything useful."

**Objectives**
- Reach Volkova at the café near the State Opera.
- Escort her across the city to the boat on the Danube. **Volkova must survive.**
- *Bonus:* find the Black Frost radio van and destroy it (Black Frost reinforcements stop arriving).
- *Hidden:* reach the Prater Ferris wheel for a view over the whole north of the map.

**How it plays.** A no-base commando mission. Black Frost patrols sweep set routes with dogs; searchlights cover the
bridges. Moreau's agents can disguise themselves (spy) and open side routes. The ring road is the fast way and the
best guarded; the back streets are slow and safe. Civilians must not be harmed; killing civilians lowers the end score.

**Ending (outro cinematic).** On the boat, Volkova speaks for the first time:

> **Volkova:** "I designed X-Strain to carry medicine into the body. Marshal Drozdov taught it to carry something else.
> You are too late to stop it being made. You are not too late to stop it being used. It is grown in a place we call
> *Kolodets*, the Well. I was never told where it is. But the first samples went south, to the desert, to see how they
> survive the heat."

**New:** commandos, spies, stealth, escort. **Thread:** Volkova joins.

### A3 — Sands of Giza
**Day 10 · Cairo, Egypt · Briefing: Hart**

**Story.** Egypt is a Union nation, but Drozdov's people got in under cover: a "joint archaeological survey" on the edge
of the Giza plateau. Under the survey tents is a front lab holding X-Strain samples for heat testing. The Egyptian army
joins the raid.

> **Hart:** "Cairo has given us permission and a battalion of Sand Chariots. The lab is on the plateau, south of the
> pyramids. I need those samples intact, Commander. Volkova can't make a vaccine from ashes. And I'd remind everyone
> that the pyramids have survived four and a half thousand years. Let's not be the ones who change that."

**Objectives**
- Break through the Vanguard perimeter on the plateau.
- **Capture** the front lab with Engineers. Destroying the lab releases a small cloud and fails the objective.
- Hold the lab while the Hazmat convoy drives the samples out to the Nile.
- *Bonus:* capture the landmark observation point on the plateau (IDEA-04) for sight over the whole desert edge.

**How it plays.** The first city map: Cairo's streets on one side, open desert on the other. Egyptian **Sand Chariots**
(IDEA-03) join as reinforcements and can cross the Nile. The player must win without heavy artillery near the lab
(splash damage on the lab is the danger). After the capture, a Vanguard counter-attack comes out of the desert while
the convoy runs a timed route to the river.

**Ending.** Volkova studies the samples overnight and calls Hart at dawn, shaken: hidden in X-Strain's code is a
signature, two letters, **E.V.** *"Elias Varga. My teacher. He died in a laboratory fire nine years ago. Nobody else
ever signed their work that way."* Hart files it as a curiosity.

**New:** landmarks, engineers capturing under pressure, allied national units. **Thread:** first Architect clue.

## 7. Act II — The Spread (Day 8–5)

### A4 — Caribbean Crisis
**Day 8 · Havana, Cuba · Briefing: Hart**

**Story.** A Union satellite spots six fast missile boats in Havana harbour loading canisters marked as crop spray.
Volkova recognises the canister seals. If the boats reach the Atlantic shipping lanes, nobody will find them again.

> **Hart:** "Six boats, one harbour, one night. The *Resolute* carrier group is off the coast. Use her jets. Sink the boats
> before they clear the harbour mouth, and take the warhead depot on the docks. I want to know what they were really
> loading."

**Objectives**
- Sink all six missile boats before they leave the harbour (each one that escapes is a loss).
- Destroy the coastal SAM sites that protect the harbour.
- Land troops and **capture** the warhead depot on the docks.
- *Bonus:* destroy the Hurricane-charged National Monument (IDEA-02) before it fires.

**How it plays.** No ground base at first: the player commands a carrier (IDEA-08) and its jets, then lands an MCV once
the SAMs on the beach are down. Cuban **Guerrillas** (IDEA-03) hide in the hills and old town: invisible until they
move. The boats leave one by one on a timer; jets must pick them off while the SAMs still cover the harbour.

**Ending.** The canisters in the depot don't fit any boat. They are warhead liners for a **long-range rocket**.
Havana was a test of the assembly line. *"The boats were never the weapon,"* says Volkova. *"They want it to fall from
the sky."*

**New:** carriers, carrier jets, naval landings. **Thread:** the dispersal missiles.

### A5 — First Outbreak
**Day 7 · Agra, India · Briefing: Moreau, Volkova**

**Story.** A Black Frost team runs a secret field trial at a depot outside Agra. Something goes wrong. A contamination
zone starts to spread along the Yamuna toward the city. The local Vanguard garrison has no idea what it is. Its commander,
**Colonel Arjun Rao**, does something unheard of: he radios the Union for help.

> **Moreau:** "We're in Vanguard territory, at the invitation of a Vanguard colonel, to clean up a Vanguard weapon. If this
> works, I'm putting it in my memoirs."
>
> **Volkova:** "The cloud moves with the wind and grows where people are. Keep everyone out of it except the Hazmat
> teams. And Commander… it will look small. It isn't."

**Objectives**
- Build a base (Colonel Rao lets you land at the airfield east of the city).
- Contain the contamination: clean every zone with **Decontamination Trucks** before it reaches the city centre.
- Evacuate civilians from the riverside districts to the assembly point at the **Taj Mahal** gardens.
- Destroy the Black Frost depot where the trial began. Black Frost troops attack **both** you and Rao.
- *Bonus:* keep Colonel Rao's headquarters standing (better ending line, extra Indian units in the last phase).

**How it plays.** The first mission with **contamination zones** (`PxContamination`): they spread cell by cell with the
wind, hurt infantry and civilians, and are cleaned by Decontamination Trucks. **Hazmat Troopers** can work inside them.
Rao's Indian units are a **temporary ally** (shared vision, not under your command); one **War Elephant** (IDEA-03)
carries civilians. Fighting and cleaning at the same time is the challenge: the Black Frost depot keeps reseeding the
cloud until it is destroyed.

**Ending (outro cinematic).** The river runs clear. Rao and Moreau shake hands at the gate. Rao: *"My generals will want
to know what happened here."* Moreau hands him a folder: *"Then give them this."* Volkova, looking at the cleaned zone on
the map: *"That was two litres. The Well holds forty thousand."*

**New:** contamination zones, Decontamination Truck, Hazmat Trooper, temporary allies. **Thread:** not all Vanguard are
Drozdov.

### A6 — Hold the Line
**Day 5 · Tokyo, Japan · Briefing: Hart, Volkova**

**Story.** Volkova has moved her work to a research lab on the shore of Tokyo Bay. A vaccine is close. Drozdov knows it,
and sends everything he can spare: landing ships, paratroopers and a bomber stream.

> **Hart:** "Doctor Volkova needs thirty more minutes. Drozdov is sending a fleet to make sure she doesn't get them. Captain
> Sato and the Japanese defence forces are under your command. Hold that lab, Commander. Whatever it takes."
>
> **Volkova:** "Please don't let them hit the cold room. It took me nine years to learn how to make this."

**Objectives**
- **Defend the vaccine lab** until its research bar fills (30 minutes on Normal).
- Keep at least one of the two bay power plants running (the lab needs power).
- *Bonus:* build the Japanese National Monument and use the **Tsunami** (IDEA-02) on the landing fleet.

**How it plays.** A big defence mission with a strong starting base. Waves: naval landings from the bay, paratroopers on
the inner districts, a heavy bomber stream in the last ten minutes. **Ashigaru Walkers** (IDEA-03) are the backbone.
Defences, walls and anti-air matter most. The lab's research pauses while it is under 50 % health.

**Ending.** The vaccine works. But Volkova is honest: *"Weeks to make a million doses. Months to make enough. On Day Zero
we will have a few thousand."* Hart turns to the map of the Vanguard. *"Then we stop it at the source. Find me the Well,
and get the Air Staff on the line. If it's underground, I want the Wraiths ready."*

**New:** large defence, national superweapons, research objective. **Thread:** the Wraith is first named.

## 8. Act III — The Hunt (Day 4–1)

### A7 — Through the Wall
**Day 4 · The Great Wall north of Beijing, China · Briefing: Moreau**

**Story.** The Chimera programme runs its logistics from a hardened data centre, the **Dragon's Archive**, built into the
hills north of Beijing behind a restored section of the Great Wall. Drozdov's own staff guard it, behind Vanguard
fortress lines.

> **Moreau:** "Every shipment, every scientist, every rocket that has touched Chimera went through this building. If the
> Well has an address, it's in there. Commander, please capture it. I can't read a smoking crater."

**Objectives**
- Break through the fortified pass (walls, gun towers, a Tesla line).
- **Capture** the Dragon's Archive with Engineers (Special Forces can clear its inner guard).
- *Bonus:* capture the Vanguard **Mega Tank Plant** (IDEA-09) inside the pass and use it.

**How it plays.** A siege. The player gets a **Military Academy** and **Mega Tank Plant** (IDEA-09) for the first time;
**Goliath** super-heavy tanks break walls. China's **People's Militia** (IDEA-03) floods the hills in groups of three.
The pass has two routes: the main gate (hard, direct) and a mountain path for infantry only (slow, flanks the gun towers).

**Ending.** The Archive gives up the Well. **Kolodets lies 400 metres under a mountain in Iran's Zagros range**, beneath an old mine,
with dispersal missile silos around it and the Vanguard's best air defence over it. Two more things are in the files:
- The X-Strain research did **not** start in the Vanguard. It was **delivered**, years ago, by a sender who signs as
  **"ARCHITECT"**.
- Several files mention a Union project called **LEGION**. Moreau asks Hart. Hart: *"Not your concern, Major."*

Volkova reads the depth twice. *"Four hundred metres. Nothing you have can reach that."* Hart: *"One thing can."*

**New:** mega buildings and mega units, sieges. **Thread:** the Architect; the Union's own secret.

### A8 — Gobi Corridor
**Day 3 · The Gobi desert, Mongolia · Briefing: Hart**

**Story.** The **AB-2 Wraith** stealth bombers can carry the **Earthbreaker** deep-penetrator, the only bomb that can reach
the Well. But every Union base in reach of Iran is watched, and the bombers need a runway where they can be loaded with
Earthbreakers unseen. The answer is a remote Vanguard air base in the Gobi, outside the radar net that guards the Well, and
the Vanguard air force is defending it.

> **Hart:** "The Wraiths need a runway nobody is watching, and the only one is theirs. Take that base. Clear the sky
> over it. Then build it into something our bombers can land on. Shade squadron is already in the air, waiting for your
> word."

**Objectives**
- Capture the Vanguard air base (its control tower and both runways).
- Destroy the three early-warning radar sites in the desert.
- Win the sky: destroy or drive off the interceptor waves until the radio says the air is clear.
- Build an **Air Force Base** with enough free slots (IDEA-06) for the Wraiths to land: **2 Wraiths × 2 slots each**.
- *Bonus:* build a **Strategic Air Command** (IDEA-06) for faster rearming in the next missions.

**How it plays.** The air war mission. The player starts with fighters and gets top-tier Union jets: **AF-22 Raptorex**,
**AF-35 Stormbolt** (IDEA-12). Vanguard **Sk-57 Felonix** stealth fighters and **MiK-31 Foxhunter** interceptors come in
waves. Airfield slots (IDEA-06) limit how many jets you have, so trading badly is costly. Ground forces fight across open
desert and dry riverbeds; a sandstorm (IDEA-20) rolls through mid-mission, blinding radar.

**Ending.** At dusk, two black flying wings drop out of the sky onto the captured runway. **Shade Lead**: *"Gobi tower,
Shade flight, two Wraiths on the ground. Nice place you've got. Where are we going?"* Hart: *"Not yet. First I need a way
through their air defence."*

**New:** air war, airfield slots, top-tier jets, weather. **Thread:** the Wraiths arrive.

### A9 — Red Square
**Day 1 · Moscow, Russia · Briefing: Hart**

**Story.** Drozdov is in Moscow, hiding inside the Kremlin and its command centre. That centre also holds the codes for
**Zagros Shield**, the air defence grid over the Well. But Colonel Rao's folder from Agra has reached General Karpov, and
Karpov is furious. A message arrives through a neutral embassy: *the Kremlin guard will not fire on Union troops who
come for Marshal Drozdov. Everyone else will.*

> **Hart:** "We are going into Moscow, Commander. I never thought I'd say that, and I'd rather nobody wrote it down. Karpov
> will keep his guard out of it. Drozdov's own troops won't be so polite. Bring him out alive, and bring me Zagros Shield."

**Objectives**
- Fight from the river landing to Red Square through Drozdov's loyal divisions.
- Do **not** attack the Kremlin guard (grey-marked Vanguard units). Firing on them ends the truce and brings them in.
- **Capture** the Kremlin command centre with Engineers to take the Zagros Shield codes.
- Capture **Drozdov** (a unique unit that tries to flee to the Vanguard helipad) and bring him to the extraction point.
- *Bonus:* destroy the Vanguard **orbital uplink** (IDEA-16) before its satellites fire on your army.

**How it plays.** A three-sided city battle: the player, Drozdov's loyalists (hostile), and Karpov's Kremlin guard
(neutral, but they'll fight if attacked). Drozdov's side uses **General Winter** (Russia's IDEA-02 weapon) and an orbital
laser satellite (IDEA-16). Drozdov runs for the helipad when the command centre falls; fast units or a helicopter cut him
off. Moscow's landmarks (IDEA-04) give sight and cover.

**Ending (outro cinematic).** Drozdov, in handcuffs, in a Union interrogation room. He is calm.

> **Drozdov:** "You think Chimera was mine? I was given it. Nine years ago a man who called himself *the Architect* gave
> me the research, finished, perfect. He asked for nothing. I thought he hated you as much as I do." *(He smiles.)* "And
> you cannot stop the launch. The Well launches on its own. Four hundred metres of rock, General. Nothing reaches it."
>
> **Hart:** "We'll see."

The Zagros Shield codes show where the grid is weak: three long-wave radar stations that can see stealth aircraft. Kill
them, and the Wraiths are invisible again.

**New:** three-sided battles, capturing a unit, space weapons. **Thread:** the Architect is named; Karpov steps in.

## 9. Finale — The Well

### A10 — The Well
**Day 0 · The Zagros mountains, Iran · Briefing: Hart, Volkova**

**Story.** Day Zero. Under a snow-capped mountain in Iran's Zagros range lies **Kolodets**: a vault
400 metres down, holding all of the X-Strain ever grown. Drozdov's engineers dug it in secret under
an old Vanguard mine; Tehran was told it was a mineral survey. Around it, four dispersal silos are fuelling rockets that will
spread the virus across three continents at dawn. Over it, **Zagros Shield**: long-range SAMs, ballistic missile defence
and three long-wave radars that can see even a stealth bomber.

Only the **AB-2 Wraith** can kill the Well. Its **Earthbreaker** bomb punches through rock to the vault. But breaking
the vault isn't enough. Volkova insists:

> **Volkova:** "If you only crack it, the vents will breathe it out. You must burn it. The second bomb, the Sunfire, burns
> hotter than anything in that vault can survive. Crack it, then burn it. In that order. Please."
>
> **Hart:** "Commander, this is it. Land in the valley, blind their radars, keep those rockets on the ground, and paint the
> shafts for Shade flight. Two Wraiths are waiting in the Gobi with the only two Earthbreakers we have. Make them count."

**Phase 1 — Landfall.** An MCV and a strike force arrive by air at the north edge of the map at night. Build a base
fast: Black Frost hunter groups and Titan super-heavy tanks (IDEA-09) attack within minutes. Snow and night (IDEA-20,
IDEA-21) cut sight; Floodlight Towers help.

**Phase 2 — Blind the Shield.** Destroy the **three long-wave radar stations** spread across the map. While any of them
stands, a Wraith sent in is seen and shot down. The long-range SAM batteries (IDEA-26) also cover the approach;
taking them out makes the bomb run safer but isn't required.

**Phase 3 — Keep the rockets down.** From the 15-minute mark, the four **dispersal silos** start launching, one at a
time, with a warning beacon and countdown before each. The player can stop them two ways:
- destroy a silo before it launches (heavily defended, inside the inner ring), or
- shoot the rocket down in flight with Union **THADD** missile defence (IDEA-26), built near the base.

**One rocket getting through loses the mission.** On Easy only three silos launch; on Brutal they launch faster.

**Phase 4 — Paint the target.** The Well has two shafts (an air shaft and a lift shaft) inside the mine's main
entrance yard on the mountainside. A **Special Forces Squad** (IDEA-09) or commando must reach a spot next to each shaft and **laser-designate** it
for 20 seconds without being killed. Each painted shaft unlocks one bomb run.

**Phase 5 — The Wraith run.** The **AB-2 Wraith** strike appears as a support power: the player picks the approach line
and Shade flight flies in from the Gobi.
- **Shade 1** drops the **Earthbreaker** on the first painted shaft. The ground shakes; the vault cracks; contamination
  starts leaking from vents around the pad.
- **Shade 2** drops the **Sunfire** into the cracked vault. Fire bursts out of every vent and shaft.
- The player escorts the bombers with **AF-22 Raptorex** fighters against the last **Sk-57** interceptors. If a Wraith
  is shot down, a backup can be built at the forward **Aerospace Plant** (IDEA-09, 2 plane slots) if the player built one,
  at full price and with a long build time. Without one, losing a Wraith fails the mission.
- Order matters: Sunfire first just scorches the surface (wasted). Volkova warns the player over the radio.

**Phase 6 — Seal it.** The mountainside above the vault sinks into a crater. Leaking contamination zones spread from the vents;
Decontamination Trucks and Hazmat Troopers must clean every one while Drozdov's last loyal units throw everything at the
base. When the last zone is clean, the mission is won.

**Objectives (summary)**
- Establish a base.
- Destroy the three long-wave radar stations.
- Stop every dispersal rocket (destroy the silo or intercept the rocket).
- Designate both Well shafts.
- Destroy the Well: Earthbreaker, then Sunfire.
- Clean all leaking contamination zones.
- *Bonus:* destroy every long-range SAM battery before the bomb run (Shade flight comes home untouched; best ending
  line).
- *Bonus:* capture the surface command post before the collapse (unlocks the full ending cinematic).

**Ending (outro cinematic).** Sunrise over a smoking crater in the Zagros. Two black wings turn east.

> **Shade Lead:** "Gobi tower, Shade flight. Package delivered, both of them. The ground is… gone. Heading home."
>
> **Volkova** *(quietly):* "Forty thousand litres. Gone." *(a pause)* "Thank you."
>
> **Hart:** "Chimera is dead, Commander. Good work. Go home."

Then, at the captured surface command post, Moreau finds a terminal still running. It is sending data, nonstop, to an
island somewhere in the open ocean. He opens the last file. It is signed **ARCHITECT**. Attached: Union design documents,
stamped top secret, for a robot soldier called **LEGION** and an AI called **ARGUS**.

A recorded voice plays from the terminal, an old man, calm and kind:

> **The Architect:** "Irina. If you are hearing this, you have destroyed my first gift. Don't worry. I gave the other
> side one too."

Volkova goes white. *"That's him. That's Elias."*

Moreau calls Hart. *"General. About LEGION. I think it's my concern now."*

Final card: **OPERATION CHIMERA — COMPLETE.** *"Somebody built both sides of this war."*
→ Unlocks the prompt to play **Operation Legion** (the Vanguard campaign); finishing both unlocks **Convergence**.
