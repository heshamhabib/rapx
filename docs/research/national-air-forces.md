# National air forces — research notes

Background research for [IDEA-12](../DESIGN-IDEAS.md#idea-12--real-world-jet-roster) (jet roster) and its national air wings.
These tables use the **real aircraft names** for reference only; the game uses made-up names (see IDEA-12).

Compiled 2026-10-25 from web research (Wikipedia "List of aircraft of the … Air Force" pages, plus news sources for
2024–26 programme status). Scope: **jet** combat aircraft (fighters, interceptors, attack, strike, bombers, stealth,
carrier jets, combat drones) from the first jets to projects in development. Trainers only if they have a real
light-attack role. Propeller/turboprop aircraft and drones are left out (except where noted).

**Gen** = usual jet-fighter generation (1 subsonic early jets · 2 supersonic 1950s · 3 1960s–70s · 4 · 4.5 · 5 · 6).
For bombers and attack jets it marks their era. Exact first-service years may differ by a year between sources.

**Uncertain (2026 news):** FCAS fighter part (NGF) reported cancelled June 2026; Germany's F-35 delivery timing;
F/A-XX funding; Egypt's J-10CE and Su-35 deals unconfirmed; Pakistan's J-35A deliveries reported but unconfirmed;
Ukraine's Gripen and Rafale are letters of intent / on order.
