# National arsenals — research notes

Background research for [IDEA-26](../DESIGN-IDEAS.md#idea-26--national-arsenals-air-defence-missiles-drones) (national air
defence, missile launchers, rocket artillery and drones). These tables use the **real system names** for reference only;
the game uses made-up names (same rule as IDEA-12).

Compiled 2026-10-26 from web research (Wikipedia, CSIS Missile Threat, official sites, defence media). Figures are
approximate published values; "~" marks uncertain or contested figures; Chinese and Iranian ranges are often state claims.
Scope: ground-based systems only (public, encyclopedic specifications). Range = max engagement range; Alt = max altitude.

Rocket artillery is summarised briefly here because it's already designed in IDEA-17.
