Game asset sprite sheet for a top-down real-time strategy game: 24 separate small clumps of precious gem crystals for a gem mining field, arranged in a neat grid of 6 columns and 4 rows, evenly spaced, with clear empty space around every clump so none touch.

Every clump mixes several gem colours: ruby red, sapphire blue, emerald green and golden topaz yellow. View: almost straight top-down, orthographic, like looking down at the ground from above at about 70 degrees. Photorealistic, highly detailed. Lighting: sun from the top-left, small soft contact shadows to the bottom-right.
