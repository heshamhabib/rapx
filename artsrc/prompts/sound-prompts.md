# Sound and voice prompts (IDEA-31)

Generated by `artsrc/prompts/make_sound_prompts.py`. Edit the script, not this file.

Every sound the mod plays today, with a prompt to make our own version. Voices are made with **Gemini text-to-speech**; sound effects can't be (see section 5).

## How to use

**Voices (Gemini TTS)**
1. Open Google AI Studio's speech generation (or use the Gemini API TTS model).
2. Pick the **Gemini voice** listed for the character and keep it for every line of that character.
3. Paste the prompt. Generate 2–3 takes and keep the best.
4. Download the WAV and save it as `assets/sounds/<Save as>` (the folder is given per section).
5. Later step (not done yet): trim silence, level the volume, add the radio filter to unit lines, and wire the files into `audio/*.yaml`.

**Sound effects**: paste the prompt into a sound-effect generator (ElevenLabs Sound Effects, Stable Audio Open), or find a match in a CC0 library (freesound.org filtered to CC0). Save as `assets/sounds/sfx/<file>.wav`.

## 5. Can Gemini make the sound effects?

No. Gemini's audio models generate **speech** (TTS) and **music** (Lyria), not sound effects. Veo video can add sound to a clip, but cutting single gunshots out of generated videos is slow and inconsistent. Use one of:

- **ElevenLabs Sound Effects**: text-to-SFX, good quality, commercial use on paid plans.
- **Stable Audio Open**: runs locally and free (fits the IDEA-24 local-tools plan); check its licence before release.
- **CC0 libraries** (freesound.org filtered to CC0): no licence worries; good for UI clicks and dog barks.

Generate a few variations of each weapon sound; we can later play them at random so repeated fire sounds less mechanical.
