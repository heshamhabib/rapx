# RedAlert Project X - Airborne Base prompts

The Airborne Base (`abrn`, 14x6, three transport pads) is a new building for every nation. Its three pads are the only
place airborne transports are built and parked (one transport per pad): Ukraine's Il-76MD first, other nations' planes
later. Each transport flies out with its own paratrooper force and drops it on the target. In game the Il-76 is about
4 tiles long with a 4-tile wingspan (bits/hd/units/il-76md.png), so every pad must be at least 4 x 4 tiles.

Generate with: `python3 artsrc/prompts/generate_images.py --prompts airborne-prompts.md --category buildings --only abrn`
and then `--only abrn-damaged` (it attaches the intact image). Or paste the prompts by hand and save the images under
their **Save as** names in `assets/buildings/`. Then copy both to `artsrc/buildings/src/`, run
`artsrc/buildings/build_all.py` and `artsrc/buildings/make_airfield_slots.py`.
