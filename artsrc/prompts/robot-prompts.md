# RedAlert Project X - robot concepts (ChatGPT)

Concept images for the four Union robot units (1-4) and their four Vanguard counterparts (5-8) (requested 2026-10-29, reference photos in
`assets/inbox/new_enf/`). They are original designs of the same kind as those references (big law-enforcement
walker, armoured cyborg, tall battle droid, heavy-gun robot), not copies of the film designs.
The approved images become `assets/units/<id>.png` and are then the character reference for the animation
sheets (infantry-anim-prompts.md).

  python3 generate_images.py --prompts robot-prompts.md --category units --ref ../../assets/units/e1.png
