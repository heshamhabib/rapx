# Nile Crocodile sound sources

Downloaded 2026-11-15 from Wikimedia Commons into `source/`, cut and finished by
`artsrc/audio/make_croc_sounds.py` (-> mods/rapx/audio/croc/). American alligator recordings (the crocodile's close
cousin makes the same calls); public domain, so no credit is required.
